# Destination guide sources

Geographic introductions and map-pin coordinates consulted on 29 October 2026 through the Wikipedia API. The guides use original editorial summaries, not copied article text. Pins indicate the named landmark or representative viewpoint, not a route, exact entrance, or the boundary of an entire region. Practical tips are general planning suggestions; check current local information before travel.
